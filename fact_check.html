Known limits

  • Survey years differ (2015-2017 to 2025); each chip shows its own period.
  • Question wording differs between Gallup, Arab Barometer ("considered emigrating"), Balkan Barometer ("considering living abroad"), MetroPOLL ("live or study abroad"), DIF/Razumkov ("if borders fully opened"), Gallup International ("if given paperwork").
  • Cuba 57%: Cubadata base is 1,090 answers, about 35% of the full 1,776 sample; shown as an independent poll.
  • Serbia and Montenegro: Balkan Barometer gives only a 40-50% range; the lower bound sets the status.
  • UN estimates for Turkmenistan, Cuba and other closed statistical systems may understate movement.
  • Henley passport counts come from a media table (S), recheck against henleyglobal.com before publishing.
