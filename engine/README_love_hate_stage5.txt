RULES USED
  Frame n shows global time n/30. Audio sample s = n * 800 (24000 / 30) of voice_en_edited.wav. Regions are half-open [n0, n1);
  the next region starts at n1 with the same state. Video parts are joined silent, then one audio slice is added.
  Base reveal (flag fill, contour glow, face, status, counters) fires as the camera reaches the country, no later
  than its spoken name. Special events fire on the onset of their key word from the real WAV.
